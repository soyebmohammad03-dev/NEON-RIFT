Goal: a city at night that reads as a working city, with neon as the accent. Everything below is generated by **Neon Rift ▸ Night Run ▸ Build Night Run**. Change the builder code, not the scene.

## What changed

| Area | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Sky | Purple horizon, magenta glow | Deep navy zenith, low sodium-brown light-pollution band, two-scale overcast lit from below, moonlit thin cloud edges (`NeonRift/NightSky`) |
| Fog | Dark purple, so distant towers were black cut-outs | Exp² 0.0017 in the colour of the horizon glow: distance dissolves into the sky |
| Skyline | Six Sketchfab skyline clusters, fully fogged | Plus `SkylineBackdrop`: three rings of tower silhouettes 1.7, 2.1 and 2.55 km out, each hazier than the last, procedural windows filtered by screen footprint, downtown clusters, blinking aviation lights. About 3 k triangles, one draw, unfogged shader `NeonRift/SkylineBackdrop`. Camera far plane 3.5 km |
| Ambient and moon | Cool, very low | Cool sky, warm street bounce from below; moon 0.16, soft shadows |
| Street lamps | 150–170 cd, 24 m range: no visible pools | 600–680 cd, 32 m: about 10 lux under a head. LED is now ~4000 K neutral instead of blue. `LightBudget` gives the 2 nearest lamps in front of the camera soft shadows (512 px tier), with hysteresis so shadows don't hop between neighbours |
| Asphalt | Damp, few puddles | After rain: damp patches (smoothness ~0.6) streak lamp and sign highlights, more glassy puddles |
| Neon | Cyan, magenta, violet, amber, lime at up to 3.6 HDR | Cyan, magenta, warm white, amber, red at up to 2.8. Violet/lime materials removed |
| Shopfronts | Interiors cyan, pink, violet | Interiors lit like shops (warm white, fluorescent, halogen); colour stays in the sign bands |
| Windows | 12 % neon-tinted | 5 % (3 % residential); the rest warm and cool white |
| Security strips (calm) | Bright cyan kerb lines | Faint line (0.3 HDR), so the red lockdown wave stands out more |

### Volume (URP Volume framework, `Settings/Rendering/NightRun_Base.asset`)

Global volume `PostFX_Night`, plus the existing `PostFX_Alert` / `PostFX_Lockdown` volumes that `SecurityPostEffects` blends in.

- Bloom: threshold 1.0, intensity 0.38, scatter 0.62, high-quality filtering (was 1.05 / 0.55 / 0.68)
- Tonemapping: ACES, HDR grading
- Colour adjustments: post-exposure +0.45 (fixed exposure, since URP has no eye adaptation), contrast 12, saturation −6
- Split toning: neutral-cool shadows, warm highlights, balance −10
- Lift/gamma/gain: slight cool lift
- Vignette 0.22, film grain Thin1 0.08
- Removed from the base: chromatic aberration (lockdown still adds it) and the cool white-balance shift

Camera: SMAA High on top of 4× MSAA, SSAO unchanged.

## Performance

Measured in the editor at the spawn viewpoint, player plus two rivals (`UnityStats`). The moon and the player's left headlight always cast shadows as well:

| | Triangles | SetPass | Draw calls | Shadow casters | Lights on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Before | 3.37 M | 166 | 4356 | 520 | 57 |
| After, 0 shadowed lamps | 3.36 M | 168 | 4410 | 569 | 57 |
| After, 2 shadowed lamps (default) | 3.82 M | 177 | 4617 | 776 | 57 |
| After, 4 shadowed lamps | 3.94 M | 181 | 4686 | 845 | 57 |
| After, 6 shadowed lamps (rejected) | 4.05 M | 183 | 4757 | 916 | 57 |

The new look itself costs almost nothing. The lamp shadows are the expense: each shadowed spot light re-renders the combined block meshes inside its cone. `LightBudget.maxShadowed` is the dial (desktop 2, mobile 0). Tower LODs and splitting the combined block meshes would make lamp shadows cheaper; that is the next performance task.

## Known gaps (next phases)

- Seen from above, tower bodies are still very dark masses: the facades need roof and facade-edge lighting (rooftop equipment lights, uplights, facade wash), not more ambient.
- The Sketchfab skyline clusters at ~1.25 km are almost entirely fogged now. They can probably go (saving triangles) once the backdrop rings are tuned.
- No height fog: URP's built-in fog is distance-only.
- Street furniture and road detail (lane markings, signage, parking, construction) are next on the list.
