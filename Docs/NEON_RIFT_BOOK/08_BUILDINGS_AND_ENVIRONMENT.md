# 08 · Buildings and Environment

> [Index](00_INDEX.md) · Previous: [07 City Generation](07_CITY_AND_WORLD_GENERATION.md) · Next: [09 Roads and Route Network](09_ROADS_AND_ROUTE_NETWORK.md)

The city's buildings come from **three sources**:

1. **Procedural street walls** (podiums) generated per block. They are most of what you see from the car.
2. **Supplied building models** (Sketchfab packs), wrapped in prefabs and placed from a catalog.
3. **Procedural infill** behind the street walls (back buildings, yards, car parks …).

All three are generated by editor code during **Build Night Run**.

---

## 1. Podium street walls

| | |
|---|---|
| **PURPOSE** | A continuous, lit street frontage on every block side: shopfront ground floor, lit upper floors, neon or fabric awnings, crown strip, facade and blade signs, rooftop billboards and plant |
| **IMPLEMENTATION** | `NightRunDistrict.BuildStreetWall` (`Assets/_NeonRift/Scripts/Editor/District/NightRunDistrict.cs:406`) walks each block side, splits it into podium segments around heroes and junctions (`OpenSpans`), and emits boxes and panels into `BlockMeshes` (one renderer per material per block). One box collider per podium. Zone styles come from `NightRunDistrict.Style` / `ZoneStyle` |
| **TEXTURES** | Facades, shopfronts, signage atlas and billboards from `DistrictTextures` (`Assets/_NeonRift/Scripts/Editor/District/DistrictTextures.cs`); sign text is baked with the in-house 5×7 `PixelFont` |
| **SCENE** | `NightRun` → `Environment/District/…` (combined block meshes) |
| **DATA** | Zone of each block (`CityLayout.ZoneAt`) |
| **DEPENDENCIES** | `MeshBuilder`, `BlockMeshes`, `DistrictKit` materials |

Details added in the city-detail pass (`Docs/Environment.md`): fabric awnings on 38 % of non-Spire shopfront segments, closed shutters on 12 %, regulatory kerb signs, CCTV boxes, loading bays, parking-bay runs and steam vents on Kowloon/Lowtown manholes.

## 2. Supplied building models (catalog)

| | |
|---|---|
| **PURPOSE** | Landmark towers and inner-block towers |
| **INTAKE** | `ThirdPartyIntake.BuildBuildings` / `BuildAsianPack` / `BuildSkylinePack` (`ThirdPartyIntake.cs`) call `BuildingPrefabBuilder` (`Assets/_NeonRift/Scripts/Editor/World/BuildingPrefabBuilder.cs`): ground-centre pivot, URP materials with packed smoothness and alpha (`PackSmoothness`, `PackAlpha`), optional box collider, Environment layer. Menu **Neon Rift ▸ Assets ▸ Run Third-Party Intake** |
| **PREFABS** | `Assets/_NeonRift/Prefabs/Buildings/`: `PF_Bld_LondonSkyscraper`, `PF_Bld_SingaporeOffice`, 19 × `PF_Bld_AsianNight_*`, `PF_Bld_NightSkylineCluster` |
| **DATA** | `BuildingDefinition` per prefab (`Assets/_NeonRift/Data/World/Building_*.asset`), grouped in `BuildingCatalog.asset`. `BuildingTier`: Hero / Midground / Skyline (`Scripts/World/BuildingDefinition.cs`) |
| **PLACEMENT** | Heroes: `CityLayout.Heroes` → `NightRunDistrict.PlaceHeroes` (podiums shrink around them so the street wall stays closed). Generated towers: `NightRunDistrict.FillTowers` picks catalog towers that fit free footprints (`FootprintRect`, `Overlaps`) |
| **LOD** | `NightRunDistrict.HeroLod`: hero interiors, lobbies, furniture and curtains cast no shadows and drop out below 15 % screen height; the shell is never culled |
| **SOURCE** | `Assets/ThirdParty/Sketchfab/Buildings/` (see [44 Licensing](44_ASSETS_AND_LICENSING.md)) |

The six skyline clusters that once stood about 1.25 km out were removed (`CityLayout.Skyline` is empty). They changed under 0.2 % of pixels in A/B captures. The catalog entry `night_skyline_cluster` is kept.

## 3. Tower night dressing (skyscrapers)

| | |
|---|---|
| **PURPOSE** | Stop the low-poly kit towers reading as black masses at night |
| **IMPLEMENTATION** | `TowerDressing` (`Assets/_NeonRift/Scripts/Editor/District/TowerDressing.cs`): `ApplyFacade` swaps kit materials for `NeonRift/TowerFacade` (`Assets/_NeonRift/Art/Shaders/NeonRift_TowerFacade.shader`). The shader draws a procedural office-window grid in **world space** (3.6 m floors, 3.2 m bays) with lights on by suite, so it survives static batching. `DressTower` raycasts each tower's own mesh to find its roof edge and adds `Crown` strips plus wash, `Rooftop` plant with status LEDs, and `Aviation` lights on towers over 110 m. Geometry is merged per 400 m cell (`Cell`) and material |
| **COUNTS (Environment.md)** | 11,229 crown segments, 915 rooftop items, 245 aviation lights |

## 4. Block interiors (`CityInfill`)

| | |
|---|---|
| **PURPOSE** | Fill the inside of blocks (about 139 ha that used to be bare slab) with fabric that has a reason to be there |
| **ALGORITHM** | `CityInfill.Fill` (`Assets/_NeonRift/Scripts/Editor/District/CityInfill.cs:74`): rasterise free space on a 2 m grid (excluding podiums, towers, heroes, lots, reserved areas, sidewalks); take the largest free rectangle (histogram method, `Grow`), split to ≤ 58 m (70 m in the harbor, `Split`), repeat until parcels are under 120 m²; choose a use per parcel from the zone mix (`Choose`), then `Build` |
| **USES** | `Buildings`/`Mass` (back buildings with parapets and rooftop plant), `ServiceYard`, `Parking` (surface car park), `Garage` (multi-storey decks), `Park` (pocket park), `Courtyard` (fountain), `Utility` (fenced substation), `Warehouse`, `Storage` (container stacks); slivers become `Annex` or `BackOfHouse` |
| **PROPS** | `Fence`, `YardLamp` (emissive only), `Bollard`, `Bench`, `Tree`, `Dumpster`, `Crates`, `Pallets`, `Barrels`, `Sign`, `Vehicle` (parked background vehicles) |
| **COST RULES** | Masses join the block meshes; furniture and parked vehicles go to the **Clutter** layer (12) in 64 m cells, culled beyond 90 m; interior light is emissive only (no real-time lights); one simple box collider per mass |
| **RESULT (City.md)** | 119 of 139 ha filled: 707 building parcels, 69 service yards, 27 surface car parks, 13 multi-storey car parks, 24 parks, 30 courtyards, 26 utility compounds, 31 warehouses, 17 storage yards, 1,802 parked vehicles, 385 yard lamps |

## 5. Street furniture and infrastructure (`CityProps`)

`Assets/_NeonRift/Scripts/Editor/District/CityProps.cs` places, per block side and zone:

| Category | Methods |
|---|---|
| Sidewalk furniture | `Bin`, `Bench`, `Hydrant`, `Utility`, `Vending`, `Planter`, `Dumpster`, `Crates`, `Pallets`, `Barrels`, `FoodCart`, `Shelter` (bus shelters with adverts) |
| Signs and cameras | `KerbSigns`, `RegulatorySign` (60 / 30 km/h, NO PARKING, LOADING), `Cctv` |
| Facade clutter | `Facade` (AC units, drain pipes, fire escapes, LED fins) |
| Rooftops | `Rooftop` (tanks, HVAC, antennas with aviation lights) |
| Alleys | `AlleyWall`: service pipes, extract **vents**, caged **security lights**, **fire-exit doors** under green signs, **cables** slung across |
| Lamps | street lights via `DistrictKit` prefabs, `LampHaze` cones |
| Intersections | `BuildIntersections`: **traffic signals** on mast arms (far-side right of each approach), pole heads, street-name `Blade`s, corner bollards, CCTV; split into 400 m cells; the six lens renderers stay city-wide for `TrafficSignalNetwork` |

Prop prefabs from `DistrictKit.CreatePrefabs`: `PF_Prop_StreetLight_{Led,Sodium,Warm}`, `PF_Prop_JerseyBarrier`, `PF_Prop_SecurityBeacon` (`Assets/_NeonRift/Prefabs/District/`, with LOD groups via `DistrictKit.AddLod`).

## 7. Skyline beyond the city

`SkylineBackdrop` (`Assets/_NeonRift/Scripts/Editor/District/SkylineBackdrop.cs`): three rings of tower silhouettes 1.7, 2.1 and 2.55 km from the centre, each hazier than the one in front, with downtown clusters and blinking aviation lights. About 3 k triangles in one draw, using the unfogged shader `NeonRift/SkylineBackdrop`. The camera far plane is 3.5 km (`SkylineBackdrop.RequiredFarClip`).

## 8. Data Core compound, gates and tunnel

Built by mission code, not the district: `NightRunBuilder.BuildCompound`, `BuildAlleyGate`, `BuildBarrier`, `BuildExtraction` (`Assets/_NeonRift/Scripts/Editor/District/NightRunBuilder.cs`), and `NightRunBuilder.Heist.cs` (terminal kiosk, chamber, arms). See [20](20_HEIST_SYSTEM.md) to [22](22_DATA_CORE.md).

## 9. The crew garage in the city

`NightRunBuilder.BuildCrewGarage` (`Assets/_NeonRift/Scripts/Editor/District/NightRunBuilder.Garage.cs`) places the **same hall** as Car Select (`GarageBuilder.BuildShell`) on W Avenue, in the reserved rect (−44…−7, −271…−239), with its roller door onto the avenue. It has colliding walls, its own baked probe and intro-only lights, and a runtime `CrewGarage` component (`Scripts/Gameplay/CrewGarage.cs`) that strikes the light banks and lifts the door. The mission spawn is just outside the door at (3.5, −238).
