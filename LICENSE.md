### Not in this repository

Some content used on the owner's machine is deliberately **not published** here (listed in `.gitignore`):

- the two Harbor Rain cars (a Ferrari F40 Liberty Walk and a Ferrari 296 Speciale A) and a Harbor Rain work boat,
  whose redistribution rights are unverified or contradicted by their source pages;
- the spoken voice recordings, generated with macOS System Voices: Apple's macOS licence allows those voices only for
  personal, non-commercial use and not for publishing or redistribution.

The project runs without them (fewer cars to choose from; spoken lines appear as subtitles only).

Adaptations of the Mercedes-AMG GT3 model (its prefab and remapped materials) are shared under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, as that licence requires. Because two models are non-commercial, this repository as a whole must not be used commercially.
